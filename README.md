# Streaming Analytics Netflix Dataset Dashboard with Power-BI

# Project Overview : 
This Power BI dashboard delivers an interactive and data-rich exploration of Netflix’s global content catalog and user engagement trends. The project, titled Streaming Analytics, leverages the Netflix dataset to uncover insights into content distribution, genre popularity, release patterns, and platform growth. Designed for media analysts, content strategists, and data enthusiasts, the dashboard transforms raw streaming data into actionable intelligence.

# Objectives :
Analyze Netflix’s content library across genres, countries, and release years.
Identify trends in content production and distribution over time.
Explore viewer preferences and platform expansion strategies.
Provide strategic insights for content acquisition and regional targeting.

# Dashboard Components & Insights : 
Content Distribution Overview
Title Count by Country: A bar chart visualizes the number of titles available per country, highlighting Netflix’s global reach and regional content strategies.
Content Type Breakdown (Movies vs. TV Shows): A pie chart compares the proportion of movies and TV shows, offering insights into platform focus.

Genre & Category Analysis
Top Genres by Volume: A stacked bar chart ranks genres by frequency, revealing viewer preferences and production trends.
Genre Popularity Over Time: A line chart tracks how genre popularity has evolved, helping identify emerging content themes.

Temporal Trends
Content Release Timeline: A time-series chart shows the number of titles released each year, illustrating Netflix’s growth trajectory and seasonal spikes.
Decade-Wise Content Distribution: A histogram groups titles by decade, offering a historical perspective on content evolution.

Platform Expansion & Strategy
Country-Wise Content Growth: A map or bar chart highlights how Netflix has expanded its catalog across regions, supporting strategic planning.
Language & Regional Diversity: A pie chart or treemap visualizes the diversity of content languages, reflecting inclusivity and localization efforts.

Advanced Metrics
Director & Cast Frequency: A bar chart identifies the most featured directors and actors, useful for talent analytics and partnership decisions.
Content Duration Analysis: A scatter plot or histogram shows runtime distributions, helping understand viewer engagement strategies.

# Business Impact : 
Content Strategy Optimization: Helps identify high-performing genres and regions for targeted investment.
Market Expansion Insights: Reveals gaps and opportunities in regional content availability.
Viewer Engagement Forecasting: Supports predictive modeling for user retention and content recommendation systems.

# Technical Stack : 
Power BI: For dynamic visualizations and dashboard interactivity.
Netflix Dataset: Includes metadata on titles, genres, countries, release dates, cast, and more.
Data Cleaning & Transformation: Performed using Power Query and DAX for accurate analysis.
